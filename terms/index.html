Preview notice · 13 October 2026

Research-stage status

SKUDocket is a concept and website preview, not an operating customs-filing service. All product records, organizations, people, invoices, amounts, and outcomes are fictional.

Informational use

The preview explains a proposed evidence-preparation workflow. Suggested classification reasoning is nonbinding. It is not legal advice, a customs ruling, sanctions clearance, origin certification, eligibility decision, or a guaranteed duty quote.

Professional responsibility

Importer-of-record reasonable-care responsibility remains with the importer. Broker, counsel, and government authority are not replaced by a software suggestion, a prior entry, or packet approval.

Unavailable actions

The preview cannot submit a customs entry, send messages to a broker or supplier, transfer funds, execute a government determination, or write to ERP and bank systems. A local synthetic PDF download is not transmission or filing.

Hypothetical commercial terms

The $3,000 ten-SKU pilot and $750–$2,500 monthly repeat-lane range are test hypotheses. They are not active offers, subscriptions, accepted prices, guaranteed outcomes, or evidence of revenue.

Sources and intellectual property

Official guidance is linked to its original publisher. The original SKUDocket artwork and UI explain this concept; third-party reference sites and social marks are not partners or endorsements. Final legal terms and company identity require operator and counsel review before a live service.
