SKUDocket website — v1
13 October 2026

31 fully authored routes. Buildless static HTML, local fonts, original SVG
figures and artwork, a real 18-second H.264 hero film, and an isolated
synthetic review workbench. No template branding or remote tracking scripts.

Rebuild the page HTML and shared CSS/JS:
  python3 build.py

Preview:
  python3 -m http.server 8770 --directory dist
  Open http://127.0.0.1:8770/

Edit:
  src/content.py — five offers, questions, official references
  build.py — authored sections, routes, navigation, and page layouts
  src/site.css — responsive composition, motion, and component styling
  src/app.js — navigation, forms, synthetic state, review and export gates
  dist/assets/ — approved brand assets, local fonts, original visuals,
                 the MP4, and the canonical six-page synthetic packet

Design:
  Home follows Liberty Atlas: cream, coastal illustration, ink, restrained
  coral, Source Serif 4 editorial type, Public Sans interface type.
  Other pages apply the evidence density and diagram compositions of
  Atlantic Ledger and Harbor Exchange while retaining the approved colors.
  Heroes change composition by page; every offer has its own visual, inputs,
  output, review sequence, limitations, evaluation question, and FAQ.
  Buttons use a shutter fill. Offer illustrations flip on pointer hover or
  their explicit View inputs control. Reduced motion removes transitions;
  the hero poster and written transcript preserve the story.

Current scope:
  Website and concept demonstration. The product is not operating.
  No filing, actual screening, funds transfer, real messages, or ERP/bank
  writes. Source/list/duty fixtures are simulated. Classifications remain
  nonbinding with HTS candidates unassigned.

Inquiry forms:
  Fields validate and produce a clearly unsent request preview. Nothing is
  transmitted or stored. A visitor can download a copy locally. No sensitive
  record upload, checkout, subscription, password collection, or fake SSO.
  Before live lead collection: configure the actual operator, processors,
  retention/deletion and unsubscribe route, and a consent-aware backend.

Demo:
  Ten coherent queue records. HX-42 is the full deterministic path. Edits
  invalidate affected review; missing origin or unresolved screening blocks
  packet readiness. Human evidence/reasons and discussion approval are
  compulsory. Reopening invalidates export readiness. Reset returns the
  original fixture. Nothing is executed outside the browser.
  The PDF is the canonical six-page fixture. Custom session notes are a
  separate JSON download. Changed cost/assumption inputs must be restored
  before the fixed canonical PDF becomes available.

Measurement:
  Session-local diagnostics are off by default and opt-in. No provider is
  connected. Only explicitly allowlisted UTM source/campaign values and
  approved non-PII event properties are recorded. buyer_qualified and
  consent_submit are reserved until actual qualification and operational
  storage exist. Preview interactions do not establish demand or a purchase.
  Defined events: landing_view, buyer_qualified, offer_13_selected (plus
  selected_offer_id), demo_open, evidence_view, override_view, price_view,
  pilot_request, consent_submit. No raw records or form text enters events.

Publication:
  The tracked .openai/hosting.json retains the exact private Sites identity.
  Source must be synchronized before publishing a new version. Keep the
  current private audience unless the owner asks to change it.
  No company domain or verified LinkedIn/Crunchbase profile was supplied.
  Footer marks are present but inactive; no profile URLs have been fabricated.

Validation gate:
  Proceed with real lane work only if the broker incorporates dossiers,
  the importer funds a bounded pilot, a permissioned SKU feed is usable,
  and legal/data licensing permits the scope. Halt if maintained coverage is
  not lawful/adequate or the current broker process already suffices.
  $3,000 / ten SKUs and $750–$2,500 / lane / month are hypothetical tests.

Brand assets and source references retain their licenses. See assets/licenses.
Final professional compliance/legal review, production integrations, operator
facts and backend infrastructure remain outside this synthetic website build.
