SKUDocket should present a focused evidence workspace for US importers with recurring SKUs and multiple suppliers. Its central story is a reviewable dossier: product facts, competing classification rationales, missing evidence, a named reviewer and a dated broker packet.

This is a research and information architecture specification, dated 13 October 2026. It covers the public website, five proposed offer pages, isolated synthetic demo, pilot intake, measurement and design handoff. It does not establish an operating product, legal clearance, customer traction or accepted pricing.

The supplied SKUDocket brief takes precedence over STARTUP_WEBSITE_MASTER_GUIDE.md. SKUDocket replaces Customs Waypoint throughout the proposed customer experience. The old customswaypoint.com availability note does not establish anything about a SKUDocket domain or trademark. No domain is assumed. The guide supplies craft and completeness standards; it does not authorize implementation, deployment, outreach or account creation in this assignment.

## Research findings that shape the architecture

The regulatory research was performed through Perplexity Research, with direct checks of ITA and OFAC pages. One initial regulatory request was rate limited; the retry succeeded. Website structure research used official vendor pages. Rendered pages were also inspected for Ramp, Mercury, Linear and Watershed. Findings below are separated from proposed design decisions.

| Finding | Source | Architecture consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Using a broker does not remove the importer's reasonable-care responsibilities. | [CBP Reasonable Care](https://www.cbp.gov/sites/default/files/assets/documents/2018-Mar/icprescare2017revision.pdf), September 2017 guidance | Put importer ownership, source evidence and broker review in the main narrative, not solely in a legal footer. |
| A proposed classification and a binding ruling are different things. CBP provides a ruling process. | [CBP ruling requirements](https://www.cbp.gov/trade/rulings/eruling-requirements) | Show alternatives and assumptions. Label every proposed code nonbinding. Add a route for unresolved questions to broker or counsel. |
| HS and US HTS detail are not interchangeable. Tariff information is versioned and changes over time. | [USITC HTS FAQ](https://www.usitc.gov/harmonized_tariff_information/frequently_asked_questions), [HTS information](https://www.usitc.gov/harmonized_tariff_information) | Use “US HTS classification” in detailed screens. Distinguish source edition, publication date, applicable effective date and retrieval time. Never use a decorative “live” badge. |
| Origin and eligibility for a preferential program are separate questions. Evidence requirements depend on the applicable program. | [CBP USMCA implementing instructions](https://www.cbp.gov/sites/default/files/assets/documents/2020-Jun/USMCA%20Implementing%20Instructions%20-%202020%20Jun%2030%20(Finalv1).pdf) | Separate reported origin, origin review, preference claim and preference evidence. A missing affidavit is a demo exception, not a universal assertion that one affidavit proves eligibility. |
| Valuation requires more context than an invoice total alone. | [CBP customs value guidance](https://www.cbp.gov/sites/default/files/assets/documents/2020-Feb/ICP-Customs-Value-2006-Final.pdf) | Preserve invoice provenance and questions about assists, royalties, relationships and relevant additions. Keep landed-cost arithmetic separate from a legal customs-value determination. |
| CSL is an aid built from export-related restrictions; apparent matches require diligence and official-list checks. | [ITA CSL](https://www.trade.gov/consolidated-screening-list) | Do not market it as comprehensive US import screening. Show the originating list, its scope, source link and disposition owner. |
| OFAC match review compares identifiers and records the investigation; name screening alone is insufficient. Ownership can also matter. | [OFAC match guidance](https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/5), [OFAC ownership guidance](https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/401) | Give screening its own human disposition log. A false-positive decision closes that specific match, not every sanctions question. |
| API access and data reuse have conditions and operational dependencies. | [ITA API terms](https://developer.trade.gov/terms-of-service), [service-level information](https://developer.trade.gov/service-level-agreement) | Add a source/coverage register and licensing gate. Verify terms per provider before storing or redistributing content. Do not imply government endorsement. |

The supplied CBP PDF and some CBP/USITC pages did not render in the direct web fetch; their findings are supported here by the official-source Perplexity research, rather than represented as a fresh full-document legal review. Counsel must review final compliance wording and the selected lane.

A material addition from the research: the CSL is not a complete importer-compliance universe. The controls page must identify the selected sources and exclusions. Forced-labor restrictions, admissibility, other government agency requirements, AD/CVD and other measures may require separate investigation. This is a coverage boundary and pilot-scoping issue, not a sixth product.

Evidence is strong for the existence of compliance obligations. It remains weak for SKUDocket-specific demand, attainable automation, incremental time savings and willingness to pay. No vendor capability proves that buyers need this particular product.

## Five offer copy blocks

All five retain the supplied intent. The inherited Trade Desk/Review/Proof/Signal/Report names are optional internal working labels under SKUDocket, not verified trademarks or available plans. Customer-facing navigation uses the clearer job names.

### Offer 1 Classification dossiers

**Proposed module · SKUDocket Trade Desk**  
**Compare the reasoning behind a proposed classification.**  
Prepare a SKU classification dossier with competing code rationales, linked product facts and explicit assumptions for broker review. See where interpretations differ and what evidence is still needed. Suggested classifications are nonbinding.

Inputs: product description, composition, function, technical drawings and relevant historical entry evidence.  
Output: versioned comparison, supporting references, open questions and reviewer decision log.  
CTA: **Explore classification dossiers**. Pilot CTA selects offer ID 1.

### Offer 2 Origin and preference evidence

**Proposed module · SKUDocket Trade Review**  
**See what supports an origin or preference claim.**  
Organize an origin/preference evidence checklist by SKU and supplier. Distinguish available documents from missing, contradictory or unreviewed proof, and keep the applicable preference rule visible.

Inputs: production facts, supplier statements, applicable certifications and program-specific evidence.  
Output: evidence checklist with gaps, owners, dates and reviewer notes. A completed checklist does not itself establish eligibility.  
CTA: **Explore origin evidence**. Pilot CTA selects offer ID 2.

### Offer 3 Screening review

**Proposed module · SKUDocket Trade Proof**  
**Give every potential match a documented review.**  
Explore counterparty screening with a human disposition log: inspect the originating list, compare identifying details and record why a match remains unresolved, is escalated or is treated as a false positive.

Inputs: permitted counterparty identifiers and dated source-list records.  
Output: source-linked screening review and human rationale. A list search is not comprehensive sanctions clearance.  
CTA: **Explore screening review**. Pilot CTA selects offer ID 3.

### Offer 4 Landed cost and tariff changes

**Proposed module · SKUDocket Trade Signal**  
**Compare costs with the assumptions in view.**  
Explore landed-cost scenarios and a simulated tariff-change alert. Compare conditional estimates across classification assumptions and source dates, with excluded charges and unresolved inputs shown beside the result.

Inputs: quantities, unit price, valuation assumptions, proposed classification, origin, scenario date and cost components.  
Output: comparison, change explanation and review task. No guaranteed duty quote, savings claim or live monitoring promise.  
CTA: **Explore landed-cost scenarios**. Pilot CTA selects offer ID 4.

### Offer 5 Broker handoff packets

**Proposed module · SKUDocket Trade Report**  
**Give your broker the evidence behind the record.**  
Assemble a broker handoff packet with invoice, valuation and classification provenance, reviewer notes and unresolved questions. Preserve source versions so the reviewer can trace each proposed datum.

Inputs: the selected SKU's source records and recorded human reviews.  
Output: a versioned broker-review PDF packet and manifest. Exporting a packet is not transmitting or filing an entry.  
CTA: **Explore broker packets**. Pilot CTA selects offer ID 5.
